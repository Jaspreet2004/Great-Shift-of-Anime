Ethics & Limitations

One potential bias in our data is that it is based on user ratings from MAL which could
have different preferences and habits from other anime communities. The analysis is 
limited by being unable to see the amount of people that have added any specific anime
to their list. We are unable to see the difference between how many users had a specific 
anime on their list in 2015 and compare it to now. A realistic next step or improvement
would be further expanding the data to beyond the top 1000 anime by rating as well as
including more datasets from other websites that are similar to MAL.
